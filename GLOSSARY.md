# Hovered Table of Content

Navigation and previews of notes through floating tables of contents in Obsidian.

## Language

**Table of contents title**:
The text at the top of a table of contents popup identifying its note or, for a sticky heading's table of contents, its section.
_Avoid_: Heading, when referring to the popup title.

**Page preview**:
Obsidian's preview of an entire note, distinct from a preview of one section.
_Avoid_: Section preview, when referring to an entire note.

**Section preview**:
A preview of a heading, its text, and its subsections within a note.
_Avoid_: Page preview, when referring to only one section.

**Preview gap**:
The space between the outer edge of a table of contents popup and the adjacent text preview window.
_Avoid_: Text padding, which describes spacing inside the preview window.
